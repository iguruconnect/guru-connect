GURU CONNECT — STANDARD ADVERTISEMENT FORMAT

STANDARD FLYER SIZE
1200 × 675 pixels
Aspect ratio: 16:9

USE THIS SAME FORMAT FOR:
- Learning Request (LRR) advertisements
- Student Profile advertisements
- Tutor Profile advertisements

FILE FORMAT
- JPG / JPEG / PNG / WEBP
- Maximum upload size: 3 MB
- RGB/sRGB recommended

DESIGN GUIDELINES
- Keep important text, phone numbers, logos and QR codes inside a safe margin of about 5% from every edge.
- Do not place essential information very close to the edges.
- Use clear, high-resolution text.
- Avoid very small text because the flyer will also be viewed on mobile phones.

WEBSITE DISPLAY
- The advertisement uses one universal 16:9 responsive format on desktop and mobile.
- The flyer is displayed without the previous dark overlay/shadow.
- The visible ADVERTISEMENT label has been removed.
- A clean bottom information bar is used with a green CONNECT button.
- Clicking CONNECT opens the advertiser's Click URL or WhatsApp URL configured by staff.

IMPORTANT
Existing advertisements uploaded in older dimensions may not fill the new 16:9 frame perfectly. For the cleanest result, replace/re-upload them as 1200 × 675 px.
