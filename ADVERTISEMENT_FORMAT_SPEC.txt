GURU CONNECT — FLEXIBLE ADVERTISEMENT FORMAT

There is NO fixed width, height, or aspect-ratio requirement for advertiser flyers.
The website scales each image responsively and preserves its complete content without cropping.

Accepted formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP
Maximum file size: 5 MB

MOBILE-FIRST RECOMMENDATION
1080 × 1350 px (4:5) — recommended when most viewers are on mobile phones.

LANDSCAPE / ALL-DEVICE RECOMMENDATION
1200 × 675 px (16:9) — recommended for landscape creatives and mixed desktop/mobile use.

Very tall or very wide flyers are also accepted; the site will auto-fit them. Keep important text, logos and contact details away from the extreme edges for best readability.
