About GURU CONNECT

Bridging the gap between Tutors and Students — connecting Tutor–Student online in a simple and practical way.

1. OUR BUSINESS – WHAT WE ARE

Tutor

Tutor–Student Connecting Platform

GURU CONNECT is an online platform designed to connect Tutors and Students.

  • Tutors can Register on our website.
  • Tutors can choose a Plan and can also choose/add an add-on Verified Plan.
  • Tutors can respond to Students’ Learning Requests and get connected with them.
  • Tutors can receive tuition opportunities through their profiles listed with us.
Students

Students & Learning

Students can use GURU CONNECT to find tutors, post requirements and get academic help.

  • Students can Register on our website.
  • Students are free to post their requirement to learn.
  • Students can get in touch with Tutor Profiles and hire suitable Tutors.
  • Students can enjoy the Premium services of our Helpline Service where Subject Experts can explain topics and clear doubts.

2. HELPLINE SERVICE

Our Helpline Service helps Students learn, understand and clear doubts online from the comfort of their home, as and whenever required — serving directly to Students.

Students can subscribe to our Helpline Service by selecting any of our existing Plans.

We have a Dedicated Subject Team available to help Students with their questions, doubts and learning needs.
