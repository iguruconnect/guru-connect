About Us

About GURU CONNECT

We – GURU CONNECT – are an online platform bridging the gap between Tutors and Students. We connect Tutor – Student, making it easier to discover teaching opportunities, find the right academic support and connect for learning.

1. OUR BUSINESS – WHAT WE ARE

TUTOR PLATFORM

For Tutors

GURU CONNECT is an online platform that helps Tutors showcase their expertise and connect with Students.

  • Tutors get registered on our platform.
  • Tutors choose a Plan and can also choose or add an add-on Verified Plan.
  • Tutors can respond to Students’ Learning Requests and get connected.
  • Tutors can receive tuition opportunities through their profiles listed with us.
Register as a Tutor →
STUDENT PLATFORM

For Students

Students can use GURU CONNECT to find suitable Tutors, post learning requirements and get academic support.

  • Students get registered on our platform.
  • Students are free to post their requirements to learn.
  • Students can contact Tutors through their profiles and hire them.
  • Students can enjoy our Premium Helpline Service, where Subject Experts explain topics and clear doubts. For more information, contact us.

2. HELPLINE SERVICE

Learn or simply clear your doubts online from the comfort of your home, whenever you require help — serving directly to Students.

Students can subscribe to our Helpline Service by selecting any one of our existing Plans.

We have a Dedicated Subject Team who are available to help Students with their questions, explain topics, clear doubts and support their learning needs.
