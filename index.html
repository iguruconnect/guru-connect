Helpline Service Plans & Rules

🎧 Dedicated Academic Helpline

Learn or clear your doubts online at the comfort of your home, as and whenever you require — serving directly to the students with a dedicated subject expert team.

Helpline Service Plans

FREE PLAN ₹0

Features: Ask any 1 question on 1 particular topic from 1 chapter of 1 subject (doubt or concept explanation).

Eligibility: Need to register on our website, submit College ID and Aadhaar Number for verification, and send generated ID card to WhatsApp +1 (555) 9565-917.

CONS: Available only once per applicant. Restricted to 1 specific topic/chapter/subject session.

PRIME PLAN ₹5000

Features: Regular weekly classes (1 topic per week). Entire syllabus covered systematically. Stay in regular touch with subject experts for the entire academic year.

Validity: From registration date to forthcoming year's 10th February (for Class 12) or 31st March (for Class 11).

CONS: Missed sessions cannot be reclaimed, re-explained, or compensated.

PRO PLAN ₹300

Features: Guaranteed session (1 Question of 1 Topic of 1 Chapter). Duration: 25 to 30 minutes.

Validity: Up to 10th Feb (Class 12) / 31st March (Class 11).

PREMIUM PLAN ₹6000

Features: Guaranteed weekly 1 session for your preferred 1 Question/Topic/Chapter/Subject to learn, clear doubts, and get guidance.

Validity: Up to 10th Feb (Class 12) / 31st March (Class 11).

GENX PLAN ₹1000

Features: Includes 5 PRO sessions and all Pro Plan features.

CONS: Cannot be clubbed, transferred, or shared with another person.

GENX PRO ₹3000

Features: Includes 15 PRO sessions and all Pro Plan features.

CONS: Cannot be clubbed, transferred, or shared with another person.

📜 Rules | Process & Conditions

  • Prior submission of Topic Request is mandatory in every plan.
  • Your scheduled topic details will be conveyed via WhatsApp or registered email.
  • Fees once paid is strictly non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Purchased plans are non-transferable and must be used exclusively by the registered individual.
  • Submission of College ID and Aadhaar Number is required for verification before scheduling.
  • All plan services are provided purely online.
  • Sessions disrupted due to technical, physical, or health issues from admin/expert side will be compensated/rescheduled.
  • Students must be available on time with essential materials. No rescheduling if issues occur at the student's end (e.g., network failure).
  • Strict discipline is required during online sessions; misconduct leads to immediate barring/blocking without refund.
  • Questions must be strictly limited to 1 particular question of 1 topic of 1 chapter and subject per session.

About GURU CONNECT

1. Our Business – What We Are

GURU CONNECT is an online platform bridging the gap between tutors and students. We seamlessly connect tutors and students to foster effective academic mentorship.

Tutor Platform
  • Tutors can easily get registered.
  • Choose a standard plan or add-on/upgrade to a Verified Plan.
  • Respond directly to student learning requests and get connected.
  • Receive tuition opportunities through active profile listings.
Student Platform
  • Students can easily register.
  • Free to post learning requirements.
  • Explore tutor profiles and hire them directly.
  • Enjoy premium helpline services where subject experts explain topics and clear doubts.

2. Helpline Service

Our Helpline service allows students to learn or clear doubts online at the comfort of their home as and whenever required—serving directly to students.

Students can subscribe to our Helpline Service by selecting any of our existing plans. Our dedicated subject team is always available to help students succeed.
