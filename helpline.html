📜 Rules | Process & Conditions

  1. Prior submission of Topic Request is mandatory in every plan.
  2. Your scheduled topic details will be conveyed via WhatsApp or registered email.
  3. Fees once paid are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable.
  4. Purchased plans are non-transferable and must be used exclusively by the registered individual.
  5. Submission of College ID and Aadhaar Number is required for verification before scheduling.
  6. All plan services are provided purely online.
  7. Sessions disrupted due to technical, physical, or health issues from the Admin/Expert side will be compensated or rescheduled.
  8. Students must be available on time with essential materials. No rescheduling will be provided if issues occur at the student's end (for example, network failure).
  9. Strict discipline is required during online sessions. Misconduct will lead to immediate barring/blocking without refund.
  10. Questions must be strictly limited to 1 particular question of 1 topic of 1 chapter and subject per session.
