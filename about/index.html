Who We Are

About GURU CONNECT

GURU CONNECT — Connecting Mind - Creating Future. We are an online platform created to bring Students and Tutors closer. Our aim is to make it easier for learners to discover the right teaching support and for tutors to showcase their knowledge, connect with students and find meaningful teaching opportunities. We bring tutor discovery, learning requirements and academic doubt-solving support together in one place.

What GURU CONNECT Does

We make the process of finding learning support and teaching opportunities simpler, more accessible and more connected.

FOR STUDENTS & PARENTS

Find the Right Learning Support

Students can explore tutor profiles, look for subject and class-specific guidance, and connect with tutors according to their learning needs.

  • Discover tutors and review the information shared on their profiles.
  • Post a Learning Request describing the subject or topic where support is needed.
  • Connect with suitable tutors and take the next step towards learning.
  • Access the Dedicated Academic Helpline for topic explanations and doubt-clearing support.

FOR TUTORS & EDUCATORS

Showcase Your Teaching

GURU CONNECT gives tutors a platform to present their teaching profile and reach students looking for academic guidance.

  • Create a tutor profile and share subjects, qualifications and teaching details.
  • Choose the registration plan that suits you.
  • Explore and respond to relevant Student Learning Requests.
  • Build connections with learners and discover teaching opportunities.
